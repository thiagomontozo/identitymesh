# Offboarding verification

Verification counts known, managed, disabled, active and unresolved identities; successful and failed systems; manual scope; and evidence freshness. `VERIFIED` requires every relevant managed identity to be reconciled disabled, all required systems to respond and no blocking candidate. `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` means observed proof exists but manual or unresolved scope remains. `INCONCLUSIVE` means provider availability or data quality prevents a conclusion. `FAILED` means a responsive provider still reports known access active or a required action definitively failed.

> This report reflects the systems connected to IdentityMesh and successfully reconciled at the time of verification. It does not prove the absence of identities or access in systems outside the observed scope.
